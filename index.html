Designed by Hamid Guedra (Language Centre, LAB University of Applied Sciences / LUT University)

The ANSVA pitch model in Module 6 is based on Monroe’s Motivated Sequence (Alan H. Monroe, Principles and Types of Speech, 1935): Attention, Need, Satisfaction (here: Solution), Visualization, Action. The Business Model Canvas in Module 5 is based on the framework created by Alexander Osterwalder and Yves Pigneur.

Some content adapted from English for Work (Business) MASTER by Tessa Laba.
